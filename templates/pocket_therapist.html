Coping Tools

Evidence-based techniques for stress, anxiety, and emotional regulation

Box Breathing
4-4-4-4 technique. Balances the autonomic nervous system and reduces cortisol.
⏱ 4 minutes · 4 cycles

4-7-8 Breathing
Activates the parasympathetic system. Excellent for acute anxiety and sleep onset.
⏱ 3 minutes · 4 cycles
